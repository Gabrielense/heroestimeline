HeroesA Complete Multimedia Timeline

NBC's Heroes told its story across seven media at once. Every episode, graphic novel, webisode, ARG chapter, behind-the-scenes short and physical release is here, laid out in the week it landed — from the 2006 pilot to the announcement of Heroes: Eclipsed, a revival that, as of today, may never happen.

In memory of Hayden Panettiere, 1989–2026.

Where to find it

    How to read this

    • Each row is one week. The seven columns are the parallel media the story ran across.
    • BBC Two's Heroes Unmasked and the tie-in radio show went out on their own British schedule, months behind the US run. They are misplaced here so a week reads as one story beat across every medium.
