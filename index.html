HeroesA Complete Multimedia Timeline

NBC's Heroes told its story across many media at once, find all of it here by week of release — from the 2006 pilot to the 2024 announcement of Heroes: Eclipsed, a revival that, as of today, may never happen.

In memory of Hayden Panettiere, 1989–2026.

How to read this

  • Each row is one week. The seven columns are the parallel media the story ran across.
  • Everything is filed in the week of the story it belongs to, not the week it happened to reach you. Where those differ, the panel gives the real date.
  • Click anything with a dot for a blurb, a picture, where it survives, and where to watch or buy it.
